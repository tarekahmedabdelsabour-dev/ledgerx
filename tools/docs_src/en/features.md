## Accounting

- A flexible Chart of Accounts, multi-company and multi-currency.
- Sales and purchase invoices, payments, receipt and payment vouchers, cheques.
- Taxes (VAT and others) and automatic entries.
- Cost Centers and budgets.
- Trial balance, income statement, balance sheet, cash flow.

## Sales and purchasing

- Quotations, sales orders, delivery notes, invoices, returns.
- Purchase requests, supplier quotations, purchase orders, receipts.
- Price lists, discounts, payment schedules, and installments.

## Inventory

- Multiple Warehouses with balance tracking and transfers.
- Serial numbers, batches, and expiry dates.
- Periodic stock counts and inventory valuation.

## Customers

- Leads, sales opportunities, and follow-up activities.
- Direct link to quotations and invoices.

## Human resources

- Employee files, attendance, leave, advances.
- Payroll, salary components, and deductions.

## Specialized modules

Fleet, field sales, payment network, hotels, car showroom, education, real estate development. You can find an explanation of each module in the **LedgerX modules** section.

## User experience

- Quick search, and a home screen with an icon for each module.
- Dashboards, number cards, and charts.
- Excel export and import, and printing in professional formats.
- Works on computer and mobile.
