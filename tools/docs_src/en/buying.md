## The purchasing cycle

Material Request ← Supplier Quotation ← Purchase Order ← Purchase Receipt ← Purchase Invoice ← Payment Entry.

## Material Request and supplier quotations

Any department records its need in a **Material Request**. From the request, requests for quotation are created for several Suppliers, the prices are compared, and the best one is chosen.

## Purchase Order

It is sent to the Supplier with the quantities, prices, and supply dates. It can be emailed directly from the system.

## Receipt and invoice

- The **Purchase Receipt** brings the goods into the Warehouse with the actual quantity.
- The **Purchase Invoice** records your obligation to the Supplier. It can be created from the receipt.
- The system matches the invoice with the order and the receipt.

## Payment

From the Purchase Invoice create a Payment Entry, or pay several invoices to the Supplier at once from a direct **Payment Entry**.

## Reports

Payables ageing, purchases by Supplier and Item, and orders overdue for supply.
