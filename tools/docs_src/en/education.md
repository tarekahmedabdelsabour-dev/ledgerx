A single module for managing education from nursery to university with a flexible structure: schools with stages, grades, and classes, and universities with faculties, departments, and years.

## Main sections

| Area | What it includes |
|---|---|
| Admissions and students | Admission applications, student and guardian, siblings, transfer |
| Fees and accounts | Fee structures, sibling discount, fee installments, collection and invoices |
| Academic | Subjects, timetables, attendance, exams, grades, cumulative GPA |
| Staff | Teachers and faculty members and their workloads |

## Fees

Define a **Fee Structure** for each stage, then create an **installment schedule** for the student with due dates. Sibling discounts are calculated automatically, and a Sales Invoice is created in the guardian's name.

## Attendance and alerts

Each class's daily attendance is recorded, the attendance percentage is calculated, and **absence alerts** are sent daily to guardians.

## Grades

Exam weights and the grading scale are calculated automatically, and a **student result certificate** is issued. In universities the cumulative GPA is calculated.

## Reports

Fees due, class density, attendance summary, result certificate, and fee collection.

## Notes

- Fees are recorded as revenue at invoicing, without deferral over the study months.
- Bulk end-of-year promotion is not yet available.
