## The idea

The Chart of Accounts is the structure of the company's books. It is divided into five main roots: Assets, Liabilities, Equity, Income, and Expenses. **Group** accounts contain sub-accounts, and **ledger** accounts are the ones entries are posted to.

## Adding an account

1. Open the **Chart of Accounts** and choose the company.
2. Click the parent account, then **Add Child**.
3. Set the name, the type (Bank, Cash, Receivable, Payable, Tax, Expense...), and the currency.

## Important account types

| Type | Use |
|---|---|
| Cash / Bank | Cash boxes and bank accounts, and they appear in Payment Entries |
| Receivable / Payable | Customer and Supplier accounts, set automatically in invoices |
| Tax | VAT accounts and other taxes |
| Stock | Inventory value, updated automatically by Item movements |
| Expense / Cost of Goods Sold | Charged on sale or issue |

## Tips

- Keep the numbering organized so it is easy to refer back to.
- Do not create an account for every Customer: the general Receivable account is enough, and the account statement is produced per Customer.
- Before actual posting, review the chart with your certified accountant.
