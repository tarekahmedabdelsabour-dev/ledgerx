The sales representative's mobile screen: route, customers, debts, selling from the vehicle Warehouse, collection, and handing over custody.

## The representative's day

1. **Start of day**: the representative sees their route, their Customers, and the Customers' debts.
2. **The visit**: they record their arrival at the Customer by geographic location.
3. **Selling**: they sell from the vehicle Warehouse, so quantities decrease and the invoice is created.
4. **Collection**: they receive cash or deposit in the bank, and it is recorded on the Customer's account.
5. **Closing the day**: they hand over the cash, and the day is closed with hours and sales calculated.

## Link to human resources

- The representative is linked to the driver and the employee through the same user.
- The first attendance record or sale of the day marks the employee as **present**.
- At the start of each month, a draft **Additional Salary** with last month's commission is created for each representative, without duplication.

## Settings

From **Field Sales Settings** set the company, the Warehouses, the accounts, and the commission component.
