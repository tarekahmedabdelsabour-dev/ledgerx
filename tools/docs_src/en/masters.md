Master data is what all transactions are built on. Enter it carefully at the start.

## Items

For each Item: code, name, group, unit of measure, selling and buying price, and whether it is tracked in inventory. Enable **Maintain Stock** for goods, and disable it for services.

## Customers

- Name, type (individual / company), Customer group, and territory.
- Credit limit and payment terms.
- Contacts and addresses, which can be imported with an Excel file.

## Suppliers

The same idea as Customers, with payment terms, the payable account, and bank details.

## Warehouses

Create a Warehouse for each physical location (branch, depot, sales representative's vehicle). Warehouses form a tree, so they can be grouped.

## Price lists and taxes

- A **Price List** sets the price of an Item for selling, buying, or a Customer category.
- A **Tax Template** is added automatically to documents according to the Customer type.

## Bulk import

From the **Data Import** tool, download an Excel template, fill it in, and upload it. The system checks for errors before saving.
