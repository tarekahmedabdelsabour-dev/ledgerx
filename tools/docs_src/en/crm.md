The CRM application tracks your relationship with the Customer from first contact to sale.

## Leads

Record every interested person or company: name, source, contact method. You can import from Excel.

## Opportunities

An Opportunity is a potential deal with a value and a stage (New, Negotiation, Quotation, Won, Lost). Move the Opportunity between stages and set the expected closing date.

## Activities and follow-up

Record calls, meetings, notes, and tasks on every Lead or Opportunity, so the whole team knows what has happened.

## Converting to a Customer

When the Lead is serious, convert it to a **Customer**, and create a quotation from the Opportunity with one click. From here you enter the sales cycle.

## Relationship with other modules

Hotel, showroom, education, and real estate customers are ordinary Customer records, so the follow-up team sees them and can contact them.

## Reports

Lead sources, conversion rate, Opportunity value by stage, and the performance of each sales representative.
