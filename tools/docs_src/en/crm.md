The CRM application follows a customer from first contact to a won deal. It opens from the **CRM** tile and runs on its own screen, in the same colours and language as the rest of the system.

## Leads

A Lead is a person or company that showed interest: name, organization, email, mobile, source and owner. Add leads by hand, import them from Excel (**Import**), or let them arrive on their own: the online store's contact form and its test-drive and booking requests create a lead with source **Website**. View leads as a list or as a **Kanban** board by status.

## Deals

When a lead is serious, press **Convert to Deal**. A Deal has a status, an expected closing date, a probability, a value and a list of **Products** with quantities. Move it between statuses; **Won** and **Lost** close it. A lost deal asks for the reason.

## Activities and follow-up

Every lead and deal keeps its own timeline: notes, tasks, calls, emails and comments. Assign tasks with a due date so the whole team sees what is next. **Call Logs** lists the calls.

## Organizations and contacts

A deal belongs to an **Organization** and has one or more **Contacts**. They are kept once and reused across deals.

## Link to accounting

- When a deal reaches the chosen status (by default **Won**), the CRM creates the **Customer** in the accounting module, with its contacts and address. From the deal you can also create a **Quotation**.
- The link is switched on for you when the Fleet app is installed. An administrator can change which status creates the customer, or switch the link off.
- Fleet adds two buttons to the deal page: **Create Rental Contract** and **Create Fleet Trip**, filled with the deal's customer.
- Showroom adds a **Car of Interest** field to leads and deals, in the side panel.
- The Operations Research module reads open deals (their products, probability and closing date) as expected demand in the Reorder Plan and the Product Mix Plan.

## Dashboard

The **Dashboard** shows total leads, ongoing and won deals, average deal values, the sales trend, forecasted revenue, funnel conversion, deals by stage, territory, source and salesperson, leads by source, and lost-deal reasons. Choose the period and the user at the top.
