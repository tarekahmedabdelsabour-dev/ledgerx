Complete fleet management, from a few cars to a multi-branch fleet. It extends the existing vehicle and driver records without altering them.

## What the module covers

| Area | Records |
|---|---|
| Foundation | Vehicle, driver, vehicle type, driver assignment, fleet documents (license and insurance) with expiry alerts, tires |
| Maintenance | Maintenance plans, maintenance schedules by kilometers or days, work orders (tasks, parts, and labor), fuel log |
| Operations | Trips, rental contracts, traffic violations, accidents |
| Tracking | GPS devices and vehicle locations |

## Daily use

1. Add the vehicles and drivers and assign them.
2. Record each vehicle's documents with their expiry dates, and you will get a daily alert before expiry.
3. Create a maintenance plan and apply it, and **Due Soon** and **Overdue** will appear.
4. Record fuel fill-ups so the system calculates consumption per 100 km.
5. Create a **Trip** with its expenses and revenue, and invoice the Customer for it.
6. For rental: create a **Rental Contract** with several vehicles and free kilometers, and the excess and damages will be calculated and the invoice created.

## Violations

A violation is charged to the company, the driver, or the Customer. If it is charged to a driver, it can be deducted from their salary.

## Reports

Document expiry, maintenance due dates, vehicle cost, fuel consumption, and trip profitability.

## Roles

The **Fleet Manager** has all permissions. The **Fleet Operator** only records trips and fuel.
