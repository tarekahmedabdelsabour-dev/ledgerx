## Employee file

Record personal data, job, department, direct manager, salary data, bank account, and documents. Link the employee to a system user so they can log in themselves.

## Attendance and leave

- **Attendance**: manual, by check-in and check-out system, or from mobile.
- **Leave**: types, balances, annual cycles, and request and manager approval.
- **Holidays**: a holiday list that is excluded from the calculation.

## Shifts

Define shifts and schedule them for employees, and lateness and overtime are calculated from them.

## Payroll

1. Define the **Salary Components** (basic, allowances, deductions, insurance, taxes).
2. Create a **Salary Structure** for each category and assign it to the employee.
3. Run **Payroll Entry** for the month, and it calculates attendance and leave.
4. Review the slips and submit them, and the payroll entry is created.

## Advances and additions

An **Employee Advance** is deducted in installments from the salary. An **Additional Salary** adds or deducts an amount for a single month, such as commissions and penalties.

## Reports

Salary register, monthly attendance, leave balances, and employee cost per department.
