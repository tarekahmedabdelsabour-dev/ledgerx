A real estate development module: from the project and units to sales, installments, contractors, handover, maintenance, and leasing.

## Projects and units

A project with its own Cost Center, phases, buildings, unit types, and units, with per-square-meter price lists and features added to the price (floor, view, corner...).

## Sales

1. **Unit reservation** with a deposit that has a validity period.
2. **Unit contract** with a payment plan: down payment, installments, additional annual payments, handover payment, maintenance deposit.
3. When the contract is submitted, **one invoice** is created with the payment schedule, and the payments and post-dated cheques are recorded.
4. **Late penalties** monthly on overdue installments.
5. **Cancellation** with a penalty, and the system reverses the invoice.

## Brokers and commissions

The commission becomes due when the Customer pays a specified percentage. It is paid through a Purchase Invoice or with the salary.

## Contractors and construction

Bill of quantities, contractor contracts, advance payments, progress certificates with retention and advance payment recovery, and construction progress for each building.

## After the sale

- **Handover** with a checklist and a warranty.
- **Maintenance requests**.
- **Owners' association** and collection of service fees.
- **Leasing** and automatic creation of rent invoices.

## Reports

Unit inventory, contracts and collection, installments due, collection forecast, project profitability, and contractor certificates.

## Current limits

- Revenue is recognized at invoicing.
- There is no assignment of a contract to a third party.
- There is no property management on behalf of others.
