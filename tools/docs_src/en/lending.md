The Lending application manages customer financing with repayment schedules, interest, and collection.

## The cycle

1. **Loan Application**: the borrower's data, the amount, the term, and the security.
2. **Approval**: the officer reviews the application and sets the terms.
3. **Loan Disbursement**: creates the entry and the transfer to the borrower.
4. **Repayment Schedule**: installments with principal and interest and due dates.
5. **Repayment**: each installment is recorded, with the penalty calculated on late payment.

## Reports

Outstanding loans, overdue installments, and accrued interest.

## Integration

The car showroom uses loans in the **Financing Plan** when financing is through a finance company; see the Showroom page.
