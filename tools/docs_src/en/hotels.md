Hotel management that starts from a small guesthouse and reaches a full resort.

## Rooms and reservations

- **Hotels, room types, and rooms**, with a housekeeping status (clean, dirty, maintenance, out of service).
- **Double-booking prevention**: when a reservation is confirmed, every night is reserved for the room, so two people cannot book the same room for the same night, even at the same moment.

## Rates and offers

Seasonal rate plans for each room type, a meal plan (room only, with breakfast, half board, full board, all inclusive), rates for the public, corporates, and agents, a weekend surcharge, a minimum number of nights, and promo codes. A stay that crosses two seasons is priced night by night.

## Folio and accounts

The **Folio** collects room nights, meals, and services with service charges and VAT and tourism taxes. On checkout an invoice is created from which advance payments are deducted. Corporate and agent reservations can be left open and invoiced monthly.

## Currencies

The invoice is in one currency, but the guest can pay in any accepted currency, and each payment is created at the exchange rate of its day.

## Cancellation policy

Free cancellation up to a number of days before arrival, then a percentage of the first night. A no-show is charged the first night.

## Optional sections

From **Hotel Settings ← Sections** enable only what you operate: restaurants and bars, activities and services, group bookings, housekeeping and maintenance. Each outlet (restaurant, spa, beach...) has a revenue account, a Cost Center, and a Warehouse.

## Integration

Guests are Customers in the system, so the CRM team sees them, and airport pickup can be linked to a vehicle from the fleet.

## Reports

Arrivals and departures, occupancy, and open invoices.
