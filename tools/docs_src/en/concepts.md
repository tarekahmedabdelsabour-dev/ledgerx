Understanding these few terms makes the rest of the guide easier.

## The document (DocType)

Every type of record in the system is called a "document": Sales Invoice, Customer, employee, asset, and so on. Each type has an entry form, a list, reports, and permissions.

## Document states

| State | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Draft | Editable and has no effect on accounts or inventory |
| Submitted | Posted: entries and inventory are affected, and it cannot be edited directly |
| Cancelled | Its effect is reversed, and it stays in the record for review |

> To correct a submitted document, cancel it, then amend it and submit it again, or create a return.

## Linking documents

An invoice can be created from a sales order, and a sales order from a quotation. The **Create** button at the top of a document shows the next documents in the cycle, and the data is carried over to them automatically. In each document you will find a **Connections** panel that shows everything linked to it.

## Company and Cost Center

A company is an independent accounting entity. A Cost Center distributes expenses and revenues across branches, departments, and projects.

## Roles and permissions

Each user has Roles that determine what they can see and do. You can restrict a user to a company, a branch, or a Warehouse through **User Permissions**.

## Workspaces

Each module has a home page with shortcuts, reports, and number cards. You can find the modules from the home screen or from the sidebar.
