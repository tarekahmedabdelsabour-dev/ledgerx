LedgerX is an integrated business management system that brings accounting, sales, purchasing, inventory, human resources, and customer management together in one place, with a full Arabic interface and English when you need it.

## What the system does

LedgerX records everything that happens in your company: from the quotation and the invoice, to payment and inventory, to payroll and the accounting entry. Every transaction is reflected in the accounts automatically. There is no need to enter data twice.

## Who uses it

- **Small and medium businesses** that want one system instead of several separate programs.
- **Multi-branch companies** that need Cost Centers and permissions for each branch.
- **Specialized businesses**: hotels, car showrooms, schools and universities, real estate development, transport and fleet companies, and electronic payment networks.

## System components

| Part | Function |
|---|---|
| Accounting core | Accounts, invoices, payments, taxes, reports |
| Sales, purchasing, and inventory | The complete trade cycle from order to delivery |
| Customer management | Leads, opportunities, follow-up |
| Human resources | Employees, attendance, leave, payroll |
| Lending | Customer financing and repayment schedules |
| LedgerX modules | Fleet, field sales, payment network, hotels, showroom, education, real estate |

> Every module works with the others: a showroom invoice, a real estate contract, or a student fee appears in the customer's accounts and consolidated reports.
