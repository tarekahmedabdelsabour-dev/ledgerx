## Sales Invoice

1. Open a new **Sales Invoice** (or create it from a sales order).
2. Choose the Customer, and the terms and address are filled in automatically.
3. Add the Items and quantities, and the price will be calculated from the Price List.
4. Add tax and discount if any.
5. **Save** then **Submit**.

On submission, the receivable, revenue, and tax entries are created, and the goods leave the inventory if you enabled stock update.

## Payment schedule and installments

In the invoice there is a **Payment Terms** table: split the amount into payments with different due dates. Overdue payments appear in the receivables ageing reports.

## Recording payment

From the invoice click **Create ← Payment Entry**, choose the payment method (cash or bank), and submit. The invoice is closed if the full amount is paid. Partial payment is possible, as is paying several invoices with one payment.

## Advance payments

If you receive an advance payment from the Customer, record it as a Payment Entry without an invoice. When you create their invoice, the advance balance is shown and can be applied to it.

## Returns

From the submitted invoice click **Create ← Return / Credit Note**. The system reverses the revenue and the inventory by the amount of the return.

## Purchase invoices

The same steps on the Supplier side: **Purchase Invoice** ← Payment Entry. It supports tax withholding and discounts.

> ! Do not edit the amounts of a submitted invoice. Cancel it and recreate it, or use a return, so that the audit trail stays sound.
