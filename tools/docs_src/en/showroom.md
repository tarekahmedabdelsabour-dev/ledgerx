A new and used car showroom: one showroom or several: inventory, transfers between branches, reservation, sales, financing, and sales representative commissions.

## The car cycle

1. **Entering the Warehouse**: the purchase is posted to inventory.
2. **Preparation**: a checklist and extra expenses that are added to the car's cost.
3. **Readiness**: the car passes through the states Draft, In Preparation, Ready, Reserved, Sold, Delivered.
4. **Reservation**: a deposit recorded on the Customer's account.
5. **Sale**: **Car Sale** creates the invoice, the cost of sale, and the margin.
6. **Delivery**: the car leaves the Warehouse.

## Financing plans

Define a **Financing Plan** once and apply it to the sales contract:

| Type | Description |
|---|---|
| Showroom installments | Direct installments from the showroom over a number of months with a profit percentage |
| Bank or finance company | The financier pays the price of the car, and the Customer pays them in installments |
| Loan | Linked to the Lending module |

When the plan is chosen, the installment term, the financier, the profit percentage, and the minimum down payment are filled in, and the **installment schedule** appears in the sales contract. The invoice carries the installments as a payment schedule.

## Connections

From the Customer, employee, invoice, or Journal Entry you will find a **Connections** panel that opens the linked sales contracts and reservations.

## Customers and commissions

Leads can be linked to cars and test drives recorded, and the sales representative's commission is calculated monthly and goes into payroll.

## Reports

Showroom inventory, car sales margin, and installments due.

> The showroom works with a single currency.
