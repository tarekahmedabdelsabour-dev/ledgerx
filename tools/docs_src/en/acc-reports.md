You can find the reports in the **Accounting** section. They can be filtered by company, period, and Cost Center, and exported to Excel or PDF.

| Report | Answers |
|---|---|
| Trial Balance | Are the books balanced? And the balances of all accounts |
| Income Statement | Income, expenses, and profit in a period |
| Balance Sheet | Assets, liabilities, and equity at a date |
| Cash Flow | Where the cash came from and where it went |
| General Ledger | All movements of a specific account |
| Customer / Supplier Account Statement | What is owed to them and by them |
| Accounts Receivable Ageing | Overdue invoices and how long they are overdue |

## Tips

- Always open a report with a specific period, then compare it with a previous period.
- Click any number to drill down to the documents that make it up.
- Save the most-used reports as a shortcut on your home page.
- Use Cost Centers to compare the profitability of branches.
