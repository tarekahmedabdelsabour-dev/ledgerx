## First steps

1. **Log in** with your email and password from the system's home page.
2. **Create the company** and set the currency and the Fiscal Year (see the Company and Fiscal Year page).
3. **Review the Chart of Accounts** and adjust it to your business.
4. **Add users** and give them the appropriate Roles.
5. **Enter the master data**: Items, Customers, Suppliers, Warehouses.
6. **Create your first invoice** and review its effect on the accounts.

## Tips for getting started

- Try everything on test data before entering real balances.
- Enter the opening balances in a single Journal Entry dated the start of the Fiscal Year.
- Enable only the modules you need, and don't rush to turn everything on.

## Using search

Press `Ctrl + K` inside the system to search for any document, report, or page by name.

> If an error appears, copy its full text and check the **FAQ** page or contact the system administrator.
