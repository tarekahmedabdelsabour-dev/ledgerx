## The sales cycle

Quotation ← Sales Order ← Delivery Note ← Sales Invoice ← Payment Entry.

You can start from any step; you do not have to go through all of them.

## Quotation

It is sent to the Customer with prices, terms, and a validity date. On approval click **Create ← Sales Order**.

## Sales Order

It fixes the quantities, prices, and delivery date, and reserves the goods in inventory. It shows what has been delivered and what has been invoiced.

## Delivery and invoice

- The **Delivery Note** takes the goods out of the Warehouse.
- The **Invoice** records the revenue and the receivable.
- You can enable **Update Stock** in the invoice to combine the two steps.

## Prices and discounts

- Price Lists for each Customer category.
- Pricing rules apply discounts by quantity, Item, or Customer.
- The credit limit prevents selling to a Customer who has exceeded their limit.

## Sales reports

Sales by Customer, Item, and sales representative, pending orders, and overdue collection.
