## One system instead of several programs

When data is scattered across an accounting program, an inventory program, and Excel sheets, reports are delayed and numbers conflict. In LedgerX every department reads from a single database, so the figure the accountant sees is the same one the sales manager sees.

## Fully Arabic

The interface, printing, and reports all work right to left, and the accounting terms are clear Arabic terms. Each user can switch the language to English at any time.

## Grows with you

- Start with accounts and invoices only, then enable inventory and human resources when you need them.
- Add new branches and companies without rebuilding the system.
- Enable specialized modules according to your business without affecting the rest of the system.

## Open and customizable

You can add fields, reports, and permissions specific to your company from within the system, without programming in most cases.

## Reasonable cost of ownership

It runs on your own server or your own hosting, and your data stays with you.
