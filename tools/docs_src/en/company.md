## Creating the company

From the **Company** menu, add a new company and set:

- **Name and abbreviation**: the abbreviation is used in account and Warehouse names.
- **Default currency**: the currency of the company's books. It is hard to change once entries exist.
- **Country**: determines the default taxes and templates.
- **Chart of Accounts**: choose a ready-made template or create it from scratch.

When you save, the system creates the basic accounts, Warehouses, and default Cost Centers.

## Fiscal Year

Create a Fiscal Year with a start date and an end date. An entry cannot be posted outside a defined Fiscal Year. At the end of the year you can close it with a closing entry, and the remaining balances carry over automatically to the next year.

## Multiple branches and companies

- **Separate companies**: each company has its own books and reports, and they can be consolidated in combined reports.
- **Branches within a company**: use Cost Centers and Warehouses, and restrict users with User Permissions.

> ! Do not delete a company that has entries. Disable it instead.
