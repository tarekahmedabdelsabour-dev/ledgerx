## Stock movement

Every movement is made through a **Stock Entry**: receipt, issue, transfer between Warehouses, manufacturing. They are also created automatically from commercial documents (invoice, receipt, delivery).

## Balances

The **Stock Balance** report shows the quantity and value for each Item and Warehouse. The **Stock Ledger** shows all movements.

## Tracking

- **Serial Number**: for Items where each piece is tracked.
- **Batch**: for Items that have a production or expiry date.

## Stock counting

Use **Stock Reconciliation** to enter the actual quantities. The system calculates the differences and posts an adjustment entry for their value.

## Inventory valuation

The valuation method is set (Moving Average or FIFO). The value is reflected automatically in the stock account and the cost of goods sold.

## Reorder level

Set a minimum level and a reorder quantity for each Item, and the system creates a **Material Request** when stock drops.

> ! A negative balance usually indicates a sales invoice made before the purchase receipt. Disable it from Stock Settings if you want to prevent it.
