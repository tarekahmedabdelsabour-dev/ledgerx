## Project and tasks

Create a **Project** with a Customer, dates, and a budget, then divide it into **Tasks**, each assigned to someone responsible with a due date and a completion percentage.

## Time tracking

Employees record work hours on tasks in the **Timesheet**, and cost and hourly billing are calculated from them.

## Cost and profitability

Invoices and expenses linked to the project are collected in its Cost Center. The **Project Profitability** report shows revenue, cost, and margin.

## Billing

Bill the Customer in installments according to completion milestones, or by the recorded hours.
